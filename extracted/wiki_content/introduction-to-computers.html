In today’s lesson, we will be learning the basic setup of a computer.
A computer is simply an electronic device that has the capability to store, retrieve and process data. By this information a computer can write documents, play games, send email and access the web. A computer has two components. Which are:
  • Hardware
  • Software
As an athlete, I require health and energy. So does a computer and its key components.
Hardware The hardware of a computer is its physical structure. Things like the mouse, keyboard, monitor can be classified as a computer hardware. There are also internal hardware parts such as the motherboard. Software This is the set of instructions that tells what the hardware to do. For example web browsers and video games.

Note: Whatever you do on your computer completely relies on both the hardware and the software of the computers. For example, when browsing through the internet, you will need a mouse or touchpad to navigate or clicking on buttons and links.

When you hear the word computer, what comes to mind? Either a desktop computer or laptop?Well you are correct but computers come in different forms and sizes as well. For example your smartphone is also a computer as well as your tablet.
 

Desktop Computers

You might have seen or come across this computer:
 
Well, this is a desktop computer. It is designed to be placed on desks and tables. They are made up of the following parts:
  • Monitor - This is the screen that displays information
  • Mouse - This is the small device that you use to navigate on the information that is displayed on the monitor.
  • Keyboard - This is a typewriter-style device in which you will use the keys to enter data or manipulate data.
  • Unit - This is the big box that contains or houses the main components of a computer. For example the processor, RAM.

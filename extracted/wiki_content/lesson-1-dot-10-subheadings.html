There are other smaller headings, called subheadings. h2, h3, h4, h5, and h6 are valid subheadings. h1 is bigger than h2, which is bigger than h3, and so on.

Add a subheading that says Basketball to your page for our section about lions:

<!DOCTYPE html>
<html>
  <head>
    <title>Sports</title>
  </head>
  <body>
    <h1>Sports</h1>
    <h2>Basketball</h2>
  </body>
</html>

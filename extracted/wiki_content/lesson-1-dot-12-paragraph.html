To add more text to a page, use a p element, which stands for paragraph.

Add a paragraph about basketball, like this:

<!DOCTYPE html>
<html>
  <head>
    <title>Sports</title>
  </head>
  <body>
    <h1>Sports</h1>
    <h2>Basketball</h2>
    <p>Here are some fun facts about basketball.</p>
    <h2>Football</h2>
  </body>
</html>

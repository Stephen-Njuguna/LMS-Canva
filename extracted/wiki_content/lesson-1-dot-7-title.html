Now let's add a title to our page.
<title>contains the title shown at the top of your browser. For example, the title of this page is  Lesson 1.7 - title.

  

Make the title of the page Sports, like this (Make sure the word is capitalized correctly!!):
<!DOCTYPE html>

<html>

  <head>

    <title>Sports</title>

  </head>

</html>

Introduction:

A console is a tool that programmers use to record the output of their JavaScript programs. 

The console.log() command is used to print, or log text to the console. Consider the following example that we encountered from the previous lesson :

console.log("Hello World");

Here, we notice that Hello World! Is printed out in the console.

The console can do so much more for programmers,

It can do math for us

console.log(23+4);

Later in the model, we will solve more math problems

It can tell us the time and date

console.log(Date());

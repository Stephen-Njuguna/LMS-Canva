Introduction:

Don't you worry! Math doesn't have to be your strongest subject to understand this. JavaScript supports many math operations, such as the following, which you may be familiar with:

Add +

Subtract -

Multiply *

Divide /

Let's see how we would use these operators in our code:

console.log(5 + 8); //Equals 13
console.log(8 - 3); //Equals 5 
console.log(2 * 4); //Equals 8
console.log(6 / 3); //Equals 2

In the example above, each line uses a different mathematical operator to print a value to the console. As you may expect, + adds two values together, - subtracts, * multiplies, and / divides.

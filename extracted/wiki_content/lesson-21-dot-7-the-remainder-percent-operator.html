Introduction:

So we have learnt about the addition, subtraction, multiplication and division operations.

These operations are pretty familiar to us and hence we were able to breeze through the previous lesson.

Here, we will learn about another operator: The remainder % also called modulus operator.

The remainder operator is the percent symbol: % Despite how it looks, has nothing to do with percents..

When you divide 4 by 3 your answer will be 1 reminder 1, The Modulus operator helps us find the reminder.

This can be applied in so many ways when programming, i.e. when you want to find out if a number is even or odd .

The remainder operator returns both whole numbers as well as decimals.

Introduction

The Boolean/Logical operator || means 'or' ). It is used just like &&  to find out whether either one of the two is true.

For this lesson, create a javascript file  and name it Logical_operator-OR.js

Say you are making your burger and you need to use ketchup or mayonnaise or both. You can use the || operator to see if you have at least one of those two ingredients.

var ketchup = true;

var mayonnaise = false;

ketchup || mayonnaise; //the console should print out true;

So what happens if you don't have both?

Let's say that we are keep our shopping list in different categories, and we want to combine them.

var stationery = ["Book", "Pencil", "Pen"];
var snacks = ["Chocolate", "Crisps","2 juice boxes" ];

So how can we join these arrays and just have one general list? We can use the .concat() method to join them. This is called concatenation, sounds familiar? Yep, we used it while joining two or more strings in the previous Lesson

Let's start by creating another variable to hold both arrays.

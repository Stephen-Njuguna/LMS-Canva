Index also means position. To find the index of an item in an array, we use the .indexOf(element) .

This helps you know the position of an item on your list.

var movies = ["Avengers", "Batman", "Black Panther"];

Here is how we would find the index of Batman

console.log(movies.indexOf("Batman")); //the console should log 1

To find the index of Black Panther, we will need to put in that string into the brackets

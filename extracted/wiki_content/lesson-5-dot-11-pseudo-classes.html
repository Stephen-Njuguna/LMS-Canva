Introduction:

Pseudo Classes means 'fake' classes.

These classes are not explicitly written out as attributes in HTML elements.

Here are some Pseudo classes: a:hover, a:link and a:visited. They're all associated with HTML link elements.

The selectors style links in their different states:

a:link - an unvisited link (comes before all other link pseudo-classes)

a:hover - State when the mouse is hovering over the link

a:visited - a visited link.

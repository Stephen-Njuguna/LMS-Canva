What is JavaScript?

JavaScript is often mistaken for Java, which is also a programming language, but also quite different from JavaScript.

This year, your web pages are going to do more than just look pretty and appealing. Any time we want to interact with the page, like making buttons do stuff when clicked, we have JavaScript to thank.

First, let's begin by understanding what Javascript really is and why it's important for our coding journey.

Javascript is one of the most universally used website programming languages. It is the language of the web.

With Javascript, you can:

Show and hide web page elements.

Make cool animations for your websites and make them more interactive.

Get information from forms in your websites filled by people interacting with your website.

Build games like Angry Birds.

Build robots.

Note that in this learning process, it is important to understand, rather than rush through. So do not copy and paste any code, instead, type everything out for practice and remembrance.

Once again congratulations for making it this far. You should pat yourself on the back, it was not an easy feat

Let take a minute to talk about what's next after this and what to expect. 

From here you will be joining Moringa Prep.  Now that you have gone through pre-prep, Moringa prep is designed to build on the skills and knowledge you have gained so far.  Unlike in pre-prep where you were using repl.it, all your learning materials will be hosted here in the LMS. Additionally, at the end of every week, on Friday, you will be expected to do an independent project to showcase the skills you have learnt in that week. These projects, along with other metrics that your TMs will discuss with you on orientation day will play a huge role in whether you can proceed to the Moringa Core. As a result, this makes Moringa Prep be a bit more intense but with the right mindset and dedication, this will be a walk in the park for you. 

Moreover, now that you have the basic skills in programming,  Moringa prep is meant to solidify those skills as well as help you grow in other skills such as communication, collaboration and presentation skills. Hence, we are bringing a more professional take on things and introducing new tools on top of the languages you already know. For instance, you will be introduced to version control with git which will help you collaborate better with others on projects. Also, you will be introduced to Atom, which is a code editor which will make writing and reading code much easier. Finally, you will be taken through various workshops which are intended to equip you with presentation skills as well as other soft skills.

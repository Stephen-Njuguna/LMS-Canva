As you continue on this awesome journey to becoming a programmer, giving and receiving feedback will an important skill to have. This is because more often than not, you will find yourself working in a team and as such will be required to either give or receive feedback on a project you are working on or an assigned task.

This workshop aims at imparting you with the necessary information on how to effectively give and receive feedback.

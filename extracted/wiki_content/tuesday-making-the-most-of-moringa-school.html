Before we get started, we want to share our general philosophy at Moringa School. Any experienced developer will tell you that the more you learn about programming, the more you realize just how little you know. Moringa School is structured based off the understanding that being a developer is not about learning a fixed set of skills that you can apply for the rest of your career. From our personal experiences as software engineers and many conversations with hiring partners of all types, we believe that the languages, tools, and approaches you'll learn here are (still very important, but) much less important than the general skills of solving problems. Successful programmers embrace the limitations of what they can know, and get good at facing ambiguity and figuring out what they don't know. They develop a mindset in which not knowing the answer isn't a source of anxiety, but rather an opportunity to learn and explore.

Let's explore what you will learn.  Our "Moringa pre-prep" course is designed to give you the very basics of programming broken down into small and easy to understand chunks.  We'll build up the foundation you need to start creating simple web pages and styling those pages to look nice. To add the cherry on top, we'll also get you started on JavaScript, 'the language of the web'. JavaScript will let you add interactivity to your web pages. 

Moringa Pre-prep will give you a basic introduction to the amazing world of web programming, so once you've successfully completed Moringa Pre-prep ready to dive into a bit more advanced track, Moringa Prep. Moringa Prep will give you an in-depth knowledge of web programming, thereby solidifying your skills as a web developer. 

Think of Moringa School as a place to learn how to learn.

Making the Most of Moringa School

You've done it, you're in Moringa School! You've committed time, energy and dedication toward learning how to code. We can’t wait to see what you create, and want to do everything we can to support your success. As you're beginning to understand, we feel the biggest part of that success lies in your mindset and how you approach the challenging months that lie ahead.

Some things to remember are that this program is a marathon, not a sprint, and probably very different from any previous educational experiences you may have had. Differently from how your experience in high school, college, or your workplaces may have been - there are no shortcuts in our program. We need to have your full commitment in order to help make your own journey a success. It’s up to you to make the most of your experience!

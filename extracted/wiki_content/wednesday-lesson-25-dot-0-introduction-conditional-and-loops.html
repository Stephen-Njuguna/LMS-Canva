Introduction

Conditionals are important concepts in JavaScript. In a nutshell a conditional says, "If something is true do this, if it is not do that."

Take for instance the traffic light systems. If the light is red, the cars will stop, if the light is amber, the drivers have to get ready and when the lights are green.

Loops say, as long as something is true, keep doing this. For example, as long as you are hungry, keep eating.

Conditionals and loops are very powerful and key concepts in programs. They are collectively called control structures because they allow us to control the parts of our code that are executed, as well as when and how often, based on certain conditions that we define in our code.

This might be a bit much to take in but with a bit of practice, it'll be much clearer.
